7. Perishable Foodstuff Cancellation, Return & Refund Protocol

Given the perishable nature of frozen confections and strict compliance with Nigerian food safety regulations:

  • Strict 12-Minute Pre-Preparation Cancellation Window: A customer may cancel an unfulfilled order without penalty within exactly twelve (12) minutes of transmission by utilizing the cancellation function on their active order confirmation interface or Track Order portal, provided the order has not progressed to dispatch.
  • Absolute Bar on Foodstuff Returns: In strict adherence to the Public Health Laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, prepared ice cream and food items cannot be returned, exchanged, or re-entered into custody once handed over to the customer.
  • Timely Reporting of Non-Conformity (30-Minute Limitation): In the event of manifest defect, damaged delivery packaging, or substantial non-conformity, the customer must submit photographic substantiation to customer support within exactly thirty (30) minutes of physical delivery handover.
  • Verifiable Refund Tokens: In the event an order is verified as defective, cancelled, or approved for financial restitution, the system issues a unique, tamper-evident 15-character alphanumeric refund verification token (e.g., REF-XXXXXXXXXXXX), recordable and verifiable upon track.html and settled via corporate bank transfer within 3 to 5 business days.
