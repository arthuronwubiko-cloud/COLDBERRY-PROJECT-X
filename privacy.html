STATUTORY COMPLIANCE & DATA PRIVACY DIRECTIVE

Privacy Policy & Data Protection Directive

Instrument Reference: PRIV-STAT-CB-NDPA-2026-V2 · Effective Date: November 19, 2026 · Governed by the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 & Global Privacy Standards

Confidentiality Notice Concerning Infrastructure & Technical Internals: This Directive sets forth exhaustive statutory disclosures regarding personal data governance, algorithmic processing, and Data Subject entitlements. In furtherance of cyber-resilience benchmarks, operational security, and trade secret protections, the underlying computational architecture, cluster topology, edge distribution, database models, internal network maps, and cryptographic key management infrastructure of the Operator are strictly confidential proprietary assets and are excluded from public disclosure.

1. Prefatory Recitals & Data Controller Identification

WHEREAS, this Privacy Policy and Statutory Data Protection Directive (hereinafter, this "Directive") establishes the binding normative and operational parameters under which Coldberry Ice Cream Nigeria, together with its parent corporate entities, operating affiliates, retail ice cream parlors, logistics coordinators, and administrative fiduciaries (hereinafter designated, collectively, as the "Operator", "Data Controller", "we", "us", or "our"), gathers, classifies, stores, transfers, anonymizes, and purges personal data appurtenant to natural persons interacting with our web-based digital interfaces, order routing mechanisms, and client-facing portals accessible at or through coldberry.ng (the "Platform" or "Service"); and

WHEREAS, any natural or juristic person accessing, navigating, transmitting queries toward, initiating session handshakes with, or completing commercial acquisitions via the Service (hereinafter designate as the "Data Subject", "User", or "you") is entitled to statutory transparency in strict alignment with Section 24, Section 25, and kindred provisions of the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, and the General Data Protection Principles enunciated by global supervisory bodies;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Data Controller hereby enunciates the formal covenants, data hygiene procedures, and legal processing mandates set forth hereinbelow.

2. Data Parsimony & Categories of Personal Information Processed

Pursuant to the statutory doctrine of data minimization (NDPA § 24(1)(b)), the Controller limits the ingestion of personal information strictly to those minimal data elements without which the preparation, customization, and physical cold-chain transit of perishable frozen confections cannot be consummated:

  • Direct Identity & Contact Telephony: Natural person full name, customer identification profile tokens, and valid primary telephonic/WhatsApp communication credentials required for direct parlor fulfillment and courier coordination.
  • Geospatial & Premise Delivery Telemetry: Designated physical delivery destinations, including street nomenclature, residential estate demarcations, building coordinates, apartment designations, and descriptive localized landmarks (collected solely upon the customer's affirmative election of the Delivery modality).
  • Commercial Transactional Selections: Granular item records, ice cream flavors, scoop/tub parameters, topping variations, beverage selections, special preparation directives, and outlet fulfillment originations (e.g., Garki, Lugbe, Gwarinpa, Admiralty Way Lekki, Yaba, Surulere, Okota, Festac, Ajao).
  • Transient Session & Interface State: Minimal, non-profiling client-side state parameters stored in local client execution environments (including localized theme preference flags and transient shopping cart arrays).

Absolute Abnegation of Payment Card Ingestion: The Data Controller DOES NOT harvest, intercept, capture, store, or process Payment Card Industry (PCI) cardholder information, primary account numbers (PAN), CVV/CVC authentication tokens, or personal identification numbers (PINs). Settlement transactions are executed exclusively via verified external automated settlement systems, direct corporate interbank wire settlements, or verified physical Point of Sale (POS) contactless terminals upon delivery or counter collection.

3. Canonical Lawful Bases of Processing (NDPA Section 25)

Every processing operation executed by or on behalf of the Controller is subordinated to one or more codified lawful grounds under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and applicable international privacy frameworks:

  • Contractual Performance & Pre-Contractual Measures (NDPA § 25(1)(b)): Ingestion of identity, telephony, and residential delivery vectors is indispensable to the formation, execution, and physical performance of the procurement agreement entered into by the User.
  • Overriding Legitimate Interests (NDPA § 25(1)(f)): The processing of operational logs, anomalous request rates, and telemetry is essential to preserving the cyber-resilience of the Platform, thwarting fraudulent dispatch requests, enforcing rate limits, preserving digital perimeter defenses, and preventing Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) disruptions.
  • Voluntary Explicit Consent (NDPA § 25(1)(a)): Explicit, affirmative consent manifested when the User marks the statutory verification checkbox prior to the transmission of preformatted orders into WhatsApp dispatch clearinghouses.
  • Statutory & Regulatory Mandate (NDPA § 25(1)(c)): Preservation of fiscal settlement ledgers, tax documentation, and accounting archives mandated under Federal Inland Revenue regulations and relevant corporate statutes of Nigeria.

4. Non-Disclosure of System Internals & Infrastructural Confidentiality

In accordance with high-assurance security engineering, the Data Controller strictly maintains confidentiality over all non-public technical, infrastructural, and system mechanisms:

The Platform’s underlying server nodes, cloud hosting providers, database clustering mechanisms, API routing gateways, backend middleware architectures, microservice communications, edge caching nodes, operational firewalls, and internal administrator endpoints constitute high-value proprietary trade secrets and confidential security assets. The Data Controller systematically disclaims any obligation to publish or reveal such architectural specifics. Telemetry gathered during client visits (such as truncated IP addresses, TLS handshake cipher suites, and HTTP request headers) is processed in volatile memory for ingress routing and automated defense, and is strictly insulated from external reconnaissance or disclosure.

5. Strict Limitations on Downstream Data Transmissions

The Controller maintains an absolute prohibition against the commercialization, syndication, sale, rental, or unprincipled barter of User information. External disclosures are restricted strictly to authorized downstream data processors under binding contractual confidentiality covenants:

  • WhatsApp Messaging Infrastructure (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd / Meta Platforms Inc): Preformatted order summaries are handed off to WhatsApp via client-directed API invocations solely upon the User's affirmative initiation of communication with verified parlor dispatchers.
  • Fulfillment & Fleet Logistics Personnel: Customer name, contact telephone credentials, and physical address vectors are provided to vetted internal couriers and logistics personnel solely for the temporal duration of the active transit run.
  • Statutory Authorities: Disclosures executed exclusively upon presentation of mandatory judicial summons, enforceable subpoenas, or formal warrants issued by courts of competent jurisdiction within the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

7. Temporal Retention & Automated Cryptographic Purging

Personal data is retained solely for the temporal duration strictly necessary to satisfy the objectives delineated under Section 2 and Section 3 hereof. Operational transaction records, customer contact notes, and digital feedback submissions are preserved for a maximum statutory ledger lifecycle of twenty-four (24) months for fiscal reconciliation, taxation compliance, and formal warranty resolution. Following the expiration of said temporal milestone, all records are permanently, irreversibly scrubbed from active storage mediums or subjected to non-reversible mathematical pseudonymization.

8. Codified Data Subject Rights & Enforcement Mechanisms

Pursuant to Part VI of the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, Data Subjects are endowed with actionable, enforceable rights regarding the stewardship of their data, which may be asserted without cost or commercial detriment:

  • Right of Statutory Access (§ 34 NDPA): The entitlement to obtain formal verification of active processing and to receive an intelligible summary of data categories undergoing custody.
  • Right to Rectification (§ 35 NDPA): The prerogative to demand the expeditious correction of inaccurate, misleading, or outdated personal particulars maintained within our systems.
  • Right to Erasure & De-Identification (§ 36 NDPA): The right to mandate the permanent expungement of personal data where processing has lost statutory or contractual justification.
  • Right to Object to Processing (§ 37 NDPA): The right to object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing predicated upon legitimate commercial interests.
  • Right to Redress with the National Supervisory Commission: If a Data Subject considers that the processing of their personal data contravenes statutory enactments, they retain the unimpeded right to lodge a formal petition with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) (https://ndpc.gov.ng).
